The mission

Land a rocket on a pad, in weather it has never seen, with no human pilot. The AI above taught itself how, by trial and error.

  1. 01

    Learn by crashing

    It started with no idea how to fly. Every attempt earned points for landing gently and lost points for crashing, tilting or wasting fuel. Over two million practice steps it adjusted itself to earn more points. That is reinforcement learning.

  2. 02

    Practise in a thousand worlds

    An AI that practises in one world learns tricks that only work there. So every practice flight rolled new gravity, wind, turbulence and engine power. Robotics teams use the same idea, domain randomization, to move robots from simulation into the real world.

  3. 03

    Feel the wind, then tune

    Wind is invisible, so the final AI remembers its last four moments and can feel which way it is being pushed. An automatic search then tuned its training settings, judged on practice landings only, never on the final test.

Results

Tested once, on 300 landings per condition that no AI had seen before. The test plan was committed to git before the test ran, and nothing was changed after.
