# LANDFALL

**A rocket-landing AI that taught itself to land by trial and error, trained in randomized gravity, wind, turbulence and engine power.**

**Live demo:** https://s4dai-landfall.static.hf.space
**Video:** [same landing in gravity 12, normal-only AI vs the final AI](https://s4dai-landfall.static.hf.space/videos/side_by_side_heavy_gravity.mp4)

Set the conditions, pick a pilot, press Launch. Every landing is flown live in your browser by the trained neural network.

*Designed & built by Saad Hilal · S4D*
